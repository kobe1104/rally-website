Prohibited Products Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy lists products that merchants may not offer through Rally, a service of Echo AI LLC. It forms part of the Rally Merchant Agreement and of the Terms of Service. Rally may update it from time to time, and may refuse or delist any deal that does not comply.

1. Products merchants may not offer

  • Firearms, ammunition, weapon parts, and other regulated weapons
  • Explosives
  • Alcohol
  • Tobacco, nicotine, and vaping products
  • Cannabis, THC, and CBD products
  • Controlled substances and recreational drugs
  • Prescription drugs
  • Products marketed or intended for unlawful drug use
  • Medical devices, unless Rally expressly approves the category in the future
  • Recalled, counterfeit, stolen, infringing, or otherwise illegal products
  • Products that Stripe prohibits or restricts, where Rally does not expressly support the category
  • Financial products and gift cards
  • Adult content and sexually explicit products
  • Any other product Rally determines creates unacceptable legal, regulatory, payment-network, or safety risk

2. Ordinary personal-care products

Some everyday personal-care products are regulated by the FDA as over-the-counter drugs or as both cosmetics and drugs — for example sunscreen, antiperspirant, acne-care, and anti-dandruff products. These are not prohibited merely because of that classification. They may be offered only where lawful and within product categories Rally expressly supports.

Some categories (for example medicated skin care, hair-loss products, dietary supplements, and food) require Rally’s review before a deal can be published. Rally may decline a deal in its reasonable discretion.

3. Product claims

Merchants must not make disease, cure, or treatment claims unless legally permitted and supported, and must have competent and reliable evidence for every objective product claim.

4. Reporting a product

If you believe a rally offers a prohibited product, contact hello@echoaisms.com.
