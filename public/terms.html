3. How rallies work

A “rally” is a group buy on Rally: a merchant’s offer that is charged only if enough people join before a deadline.

Merchant-published terms

Each active rally must be based on terms published by the merchant. Those terms include the eligible product, rally price, the number of units needed for the rally to fill (the “goal”), the deadline, shipping and fulfillment terms, and the merchant’s return and refund policy. The rally page shows these terms before you join. A rally’s terms are fixed once it opens and do not change while it is open.

Only merchants open rallies, and only for deals with merchant-published terms. A merchant may open an official rally for its own offer. A Merchant may open a rally for a Creator Partner to promote; the creator partner may then share that merchant-created rally. Creator partners do not have Rally accounts and cannot create, change, or manage a rally.

Joining a rally: card authorization

When you join a rally, you enter your card through our payment processor, Stripe, and your card is authorized (a temporary hold) for the total amount shown, which includes shipping and any applicable sales tax. You are not charged when you join. A rally counts the number of units covered by successful authorizations; clicks, shares, unfinished checkouts, and declined or cancelled authorizations do not count.

If the rally fills

If the rally reaches its goal by the deadline, the rally fills and your card is charged the authorized amount through Stripe, for your purchase from the merchant, shortly after the deadline. A rally that reaches its goal early stays open until the deadline so others can join. The merchant then fulfills your order under its published shipping, return, and refund terms.

If the rally does not fill

If the rally does not reach its goal by the deadline, or it is cancelled by the merchant or by Rally, you are not charged. We ask Stripe to cancel the hold right away. Your bank controls when the pending amount disappears from your account, which usually takes 5–10 business days. If a hold is still showing after that, contact your bank and tell them the authorization was cancelled; you may also contact us.

If a charge fails

If your card cannot be charged when a rally fills (for example, the authorization expired or your bank declined it), you will not receive the order and will not be charged. The rally still fills for the other participants.

Joining is final

Rallies work only if everyone who joins stays in until the deadline, so the merchant can offer the rally price. Once you join a rally, you cannot cancel your spot or withdraw your authorization through Rally. Before you can join, the rally page asks you to confirm that you understand this and the amount that will be charged if the rally fills.
