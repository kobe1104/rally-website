Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Echo AI LLC (“Echo AI,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides Rally, a group-buying service for merchants, organizers, and shoppers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the Rally website, Rally services, and the Rally connector for Muse (collectively, the “Services”).

Summary for shoppers

  • When you join a rally (a group buy on Rally) we collect your name, email address, shipping address, and the item and quantity you chose. Your card details go directly to our payment processor, Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
  • The organizer who shared the rally never receives your name, email address, or shipping address, and neither do other shoppers. Organizers see only rally progress, such as the number of units joined.
  • The merchant receives your name, email address, shipping address, and order details only if the rally fills, so it can ship your order and handle returns. If the rally does not fill, the merchant does not receive them.
  • We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

We may collect:

  • Account and contact information: Such as your name, email address, account role, business name, and login or authentication details.
  • Merchant information: Such as store details, product and deal information, rally targets, deadlines, commission terms, fulfillment terms, and merchant dashboard settings.
  • Rally information: Such as rallies created or joined, deal selections, quantity, rally status, authorization and charge status, the version of our Terms you agreed to and when, communications, and organizer activity.
  • Order-related information: Such as your name, email address, shipping address, the product, quantity, price, payment status, and merchant.
  • Support information: The content of messages, requests, and other information you send when you contact us.

Payment information

Card payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. on the merchant’s Stripe account. You enter your card details in a form provided by Stripe, and Stripe processes them under its own privacy policy. Rally never receives or stores full payment-card numbers and does not take possession of purchase funds. Rally receives limited transaction information from Stripe, such as authorization and charge status, amount, and a transaction reference, so it can track rally progress and provide the Services.

Information collected automatically

When you use the Services, we may collect device and usage information, including IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages viewed, actions taken, referring pages, approximate location derived from IP address, and dates and times of access. We may use cookies and similar technologies for authentication, security, preferences, service operation, and analytics.

Information from connected services

If you connect Rally to Muse or another compatible service, we may receive the account identifiers, instructions, and contextual information needed to complete your request. We receive only the information made available through that connection and your use of it.

2. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • Provide, maintain, secure, and improve the Services.
  • Create and manage accounts, deals, rallies, and merchant dashboards.
  • Count authorized units and determine whether published rally goals are met.
  • Have the merchant charge your card if the rally fills and cancel your card hold if it does not.
  • Send service messages, deadline reminders, support replies, and security notices.
  • Display merchant-published deal terms and commission disclosures.
  • Produce analytics about a merchant’s own deals, including filled and unfilled rallies, progress toward targets, and product performance.
  • Complete requests made through Muse or another connected service.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • Comply with law, enforce agreements, and protect users, Echo AI, and others.
  • Create aggregated or de-identified information that does not reasonably identify an individual.

Rally analytics present observed activity and outcomes. They do not make definitive claims about why a rally succeeded or failed.

3. How we disclose information

We may disclose information as follows:

  • To merchants: When a rally fills, we provide the merchant with each charged shopper’s name, email address, shipping address, and order details so it can fulfill orders, provide customer service, and handle returns, refunds, and disputes. Before a rally fills, the merchant sees only counts and totals. Under our Merchant Agreement, merchants may use this information only for those purposes and to meet legal obligations, and may not use it for marketing without a separate legal basis, or sell it.
  • To organizers: Organizers (such as creators who share a rally) receive rally progress, unit counts, and sales totals used to calculate their commission. They never receive shoppers’ names, email addresses, or shipping addresses.
  • To Muse or another connected service: When you connect Rally and make a request, we exchange the minimum rally, deal, or account information reasonably needed to complete that request. The Rally connector never returns shoppers’ names, email addresses, shipping addresses, or payment details. Your use of the connected service is also subject to that provider’s terms and privacy notice.
  • To service providers: We use vendors that process information for us under contractual restrictions, including Stripe (payment processing), Google Cloud (hosting and databases, in the United States), and Resend (transactional email), as well as other hosting, security, and support providers.
  • For legal and safety reasons: We may disclose information if required by law or if reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, property, users, or the integrity of the Services.
  • For a business transaction: Information may be disclosed in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.
  • With your direction or consent: We may disclose information when you ask us to or otherwise consent.

4. Merchant data separation

A merchant can access rally, order, and performance data for that merchant’s own deals only. Rally does not provide a merchant with another merchant’s identifiable customer, deal, or performance data. We may use aggregated or de-identified information across the Services when it does not reasonably identify a person or reveal another merchant’s confidential business information.

Merchants receive personal information as independent sellers and may use it under their own privacy notices. Contact the relevant merchant about its handling of order, fulfillment, return, or customer-service information.

5. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, protect the Services, and meet legal obligations. In particular:

  • Shipping addresses are deleted automatically 120 days after a rally closes if you were charged (so the merchant can handle delivery issues, returns, and disputes), and 30 days after it closes if you were not charged.
  • Names, email addresses, and order and consent records are kept as transaction records for as long as needed for accounting, tax, dispute, and legal purposes, generally up to seven years.
  • Shipping addresses are stored encrypted, and access is limited to the merchant for a filled rally and to Rally staff who need it to provide support.

A merchant that received your information for a filled rally keeps its own copy under its own privacy notice and retention practices. We may retain aggregated or de-identified information where permitted by law.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Consumer health data (Washington)

Some products offered through Rally, such as personal-care or wellness products, could reveal information about a person’s health. Where Washington’s My Health My Data Act applies, we collect and share such information only as necessary to provide the purchase you request (processing your order and sending it to the merchant for a filled rally). We do not sell it or use it for advertising. You may exercise the rights described below, including the right to access and delete this information and to learn which third parties received it, by contacting us.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To make a privacy request, email hello@echoaisms.com. We may need to verify your identity and authority before completing a request. We will respond as required by applicable law. You may also manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect service functionality.

For information held by a merchant as the seller, contact that merchant directly. We may direct merchant-specific requests to the relevant merchant.

9. Children’s privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

10. International use

Echo AI is based in the United States. If you access the Services from another country, your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where we or our service providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards and law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Services change. We will post the updated policy with a revised “Last updated” date and provide additional notice when required by law.
