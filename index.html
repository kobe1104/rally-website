A clearer way to run a group buy

Merchants set the terms. Organizers share the opportunity. Rally keeps the group status clear from the first paid order to the final result.

Publish the terms

The merchant defines the eligible product, group price, minimum paid-participant goal, deadline, fulfillment terms, and any organizer reward.

Create and share

A merchant can open an official group, or an organizer can start a group from a deal with merchant-published terms. Rally creates one link that can be shared in WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, email, or any community.

Reach the goal

Only paid orders count toward the goal. If the group fills, the merchant processes and fulfills the orders under the published terms. If it does not fill, there is no final charge; any temporary authorization is released by the merchant or its payment provider.

Track real participation

Review each result

FAQ

Who can start a group buy?

A merchant can launch an official group for its own offer. An organizer can start a group only from a deal with merchant-published group terms. If no terms are available, the organizer may submit an expression of interest, but no discount or group outcome is promised until the merchant publishes the offer.

Where does the money go?

Participants pay the merchant or the merchant’s payment provider directly. Rally does not take possession of purchase funds, and organizers never collect participant payments.

What happens if the group does not reach its goal?

Only paid orders count toward the goal. If the group does not fill before the deadline, there is no final charge under the group-buy terms. A temporary payment authorization may appear and will be released by the merchant or its payment provider according to their processing timelines.

Do participants need to download an app?

No. Participants can open the shared link, review the merchant’s terms, and complete the merchant’s checkout flow. Organizers and merchants can use Rally through supported interfaces, including the Rally connector for Muse.

Who handles shipping, returns, and organizer rewards?

The merchant is the seller and is responsible for product information, checkout, fulfillment, customer service, returns, refunds, and any organizer reward it publishes. Rally displays the terms and group status but does not sell or fulfill the product.
