FAQ

Who can start a rally?

The brand. It opens an official rally for its own deal, or opens a rally for a creator partner to promote, so the brand always sets the price, threshold, and deadline.

Do I need an influencer to run a rally?

No. Many brands run rallies directly for their own followers. If you work with creators, just hand them the link — commissions are tracked automatically.

Where does the money go?

Payments are processed through Stripe Connect. Rally deducts its platform fee and any sales tax it must collect and remit as a marketplace facilitator, and the rest is transferred to the merchant’s Stripe account. Creator partners never collect participant payments.

Who collects sales tax?

Where required by law, Rally calculates, collects, and remits sales tax as a marketplace facilitator. Tax is included in the rally price, so buyers see one total.

What happens if a rally doesn’t fill?

Nothing. No one is charged and all payment holds are released.

Do participants need to download an app?

No. Participants can open the shared link, review the merchant’s terms, and authorize their card on the rally page. Merchants can use Rally through supported interfaces, including the Rally connector for Muse.

Who handles shipping, returns, and creator commissions?

The merchant is the seller and is responsible for product information, fulfillment, customer service, returns, refunds, and any commission it agrees with a creator partner. Rally displays the terms and rally status but does not sell or fulfill the product.
